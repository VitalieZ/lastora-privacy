Lastora

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Lastora helps you keep track of the warranties on things in your home and reminds you before they expire. The app works without a server and without an account: we do not collect, receive or see your data.

Who we are

Lastora is developed in the Republic of Moldova. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at snapsatchel@gmail.com.

What data the app uses

Only the information you enter yourself: item names, purchase dates, room, store, warranty periods, service dates, reminder settings and, if you choose, a photo of the warranty certificate. All of it is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage.

We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, ID or any other personal document. There is no user account.

What we don't do

  • We have no servers and never send your data anywhere.
  • We use no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cookies or tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders about expiring warranties and service dates. They are scheduled locally, on the phone.
  • Camera and photo library: only to attach a photo of a warranty certificate, when you choose to. The photo stays on your phone.
  • Files: only when you export or import your list.

Backups and export

The app keeps a few recent copies of your data on the phone so you can recover them if something goes wrong.

Your phone's own system backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include the app's data if you have turned it on. That backup lives in your own Apple or Google account and is handled under their privacy policies; we have no access to it.

If you use Export, the app creates a file with your list and photos that you share wherever you choose. From then on, that file is under your control.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone for as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete a single item at any time, or everything from Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the phone. Copies in your system backup or in files you exported are managed by you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data. Because we never receive or store your data, you exercise these rights directly in the app. You can also contact us at the address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country. In the EU and EEA, this is the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work.

Users outside the EU

This policy applies to everyone who uses Lastora, wherever they are. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to sell or share. In particular, for users in California, we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and does not collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if we add a feature that sends data off the phone), we will update this page and the date above and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent in the app before the change applies.
